A scheduled transaction repeats on its own, like rent, a subscription or a paycheck. Bow enters it on its date, and a scheduled expense counts toward its envelope's [target](/targets).

## Add one

Tap **+**, fill in the transaction and turn on **Recurring**. Choose how often it repeats and when it [ends](/payment-plans#end-a-schedule). A transaction with a future date is scheduled too.

## See them all

In Spending, tap **Scheduled Transactions**. Use the filters at the top to narrow the list, search by payee, notes, envelope or account, and sort by next date, amount or payee. Tap **Show Calendar** to see what's coming up by day.

Tap a scheduled transaction, in the list or the calendar, to open its details. On [iPhone Duo](/iphone-duo) open, they show beside the list.

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every scheduled transaction. |
| **Recurring** | Repeating expenses, with what they cost a month and a year, grouped by budget group. |
| **Expense**, **Inflow**, **Transfer** | Only that type. |
| **Ending** | Ones that end after a number of payments or on a date, like [payment plans](/payment-plans). |
| **Paused** | Ones you've paused. |

## What your subscriptions cost

Choose **Recurring** to see what your recurring charges add up to: the total a month and a year, then each budget group with its own monthly amount. Weekly, yearly and other charges count as their average month. Paused charges aren't counted. The total follows your search, so searching a payee shows what it costs.

## Skip, pause or resume

Swipe left on a scheduled transaction and tap **Skip Next** to leave out just its next date, or **Pause** to stop it until you tap **Resume**. Dates that pass while it's paused aren't entered. You can do the same from a schedule's details. A plan that ends also has **Pay Off Early** in its details, to record what's left in one payment.

## Change one date

Open a scheduled transaction. Under **Coming up**, tap a date to change it, or its amount, without changing the others. For a plan that ends, you can change that payment's amount. Swipe left on a date to **Skip** it, or right to **Enter Now** and add it today.

Coming up lists the next six dates. Tap **Show More Dates** to see every date for a year ahead.

In the calendar, touch and hold a payment to choose **Edit This Payment** or **Skip**, for just that date. Tapping it still opens the whole schedule.

## Unskip a date

Skipped dates stay in a schedule's details, crossed out. Tap one and choose **Unskip Date** to bring it back. A date still to come is scheduled again, and one that's already due is entered right away, or linked to a matching transaction already in your budget.

## Change the next date or how it repeats

For a schedule that never ends, open it and tap **Edit Schedule**. Change **Next** to move its next date, or **Repeats** to change how often it happens. The change applies from the next payment on, and payments already recorded stay where they are. Before you move the dates, enter or skip any dates still waiting, so bank sync can match them.

For a schedule that ends, like a [payment plan](/payment-plans), the dates and how it repeats stay as they are once a payment is recorded or a date is skipped.

## Finished plans

When a plan's last date passes, it moves to the **Finished** section at the bottom of the list. Its payments stay in your budget.

<Tip>
  To be reminded before each one, turn on **Scheduled Transactions** in **Settings › Notifications**. See [Notifications](/notifications).
</Tip>
