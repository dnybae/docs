An envelope is overspent when you spend more than it holds. It shows a negative amount on Budget. If a collapsed group has an overspent envelope, a mark appears next to the group's name: red for cash, orange when it's all on credit.

## Cash and credit

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cash or debit: red" icon="money-bill">
    If it's still uncovered when the month ends, it comes out of next month's Ready to Assign.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit card: orange" icon="credit-card">
    If you don't cover it, it becomes card debt instead of taking money from other envelopes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Turn on **Snooze Targets When Covering** in **Settings › Budget** to snooze the target of any envelope a cover leaves short, for that month.
</Tip>

If an expense will leave its envelope overspent, Bow tells you before you add it. You can still add it and cover the envelope afterward.
