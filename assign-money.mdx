<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Assign Money">
    Tap the **Ready to Assign** bar above the tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill your envelopes">
    Assign Money lists the envelopes that need money, including card payments. Type an amount for each, or tap **Fund All Needs** when Ready to Assign covers them all. It shows the total it will assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Left to assign** shows what's still free. Tap the checkmark to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you type more than you have, Bow says how much you can add and offers **Use Remaining**. To undo Fund All Needs, tap **All needs funded**. Switch between **Needs Money** and **All** at the top to see every envelope.

## One envelope at a time

Open an envelope from Budget and tap **Move Money**. When Ready to Assign has money, it starts there. Otherwise it starts with sending this envelope's money back to Ready to Assign, or, if the envelope is empty, asks which envelope to take money from.

## Move money between envelopes

In Move Money, tap **From** or **To** to choose any envelope, or tap the arrows to swap the direction. It shows each balance before and after, and only moves money once you've chosen where it comes from.

## Over budget

If you assign more than you have, the Ready to Assign bar turns red and reads **Over budget**. Tap it to open Move Money, which shows how far over you are and why. **To** is already Ready to Assign: choose **From**, the envelope to move money back from. If no envelope has money, add income to cover it.

## Budget's actions

At the bottom of Budget, under **November at a glance** (named for the month you're viewing), three tiles show what each would move back to Ready to Assign. Tap one to review every envelope first: what moves and what each will have after. Then tap **Move Money**.

- **Reduce Overfunding** moves money above each envelope's target. It counts money left from earlier months, and never takes what upcoming scheduled transactions need. With a **Set aside** target, money saved in past months stays.
- **Reset Available** moves everything your envelopes have, except overspent ones.
- **Reset Assigned** sets this month's assigned amounts back to zero. Money already spent goes back too, so the review tells you which envelopes it leaves overspent.

Hidden envelopes and card payments keep their money. Past months don't show the tiles. The message afterward offers **Undo**.

## Undo and Recent Moves

Changed your mind? Tap **⋯** on Budget and choose **Undo** or **Redo**. They work on changes made since you opened Bow, including money moves, edits and deletes. Each names the step it will undo. **Recent Moves**, in the same menu, lists every assignment and move across your budget, newest first. See [Undo and Recently Deleted](/undo-and-recently-deleted).

## Other months

Use the arrows at the top of Budget, or swipe sideways, to look back or plan up to 12 months ahead. The next month opens once this one has money assigned or money in Ready to Assign. Tap **Today** at the bottom of the screen to come back to this month.

<Note>
  Past months are view only.
</Note>
