## Add a payment plan

<Info>
  Reading a payment plan needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Without it, use [Split Into Payments](#split-a-bill-into-payments) or set up the schedule yourself. See [End a schedule](#end-a-schedule).
</Info>

Take a screenshot of your plan in the Klarna, Affirm, Afterpay, Zip, Sezzle or PayPal app, or of a billing page that spells out your plan, like "$50 on the 6th of each month, balance of $380.81". Then bring it into Bow:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Share it">
    Share the screenshot and choose **Add to Bow**. It waits at the top of Spending under **Shared to Bow**, marked **Payment plan**. Tap it to review.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a new expense">
    Tap **+**, choose **Expense**, then tap **✨** next to **Add**, choose **Choose Photo** and pick the screenshot.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Bow fills in the next payment and repeats it until the last one. Under **Payments**, check each payment and the total. Amounts Bow worked out itself are marked **Worked out by Bow**, and you can change any of them.

## Payments already made

If the screenshot shows payments you've already made, like one at checkout, they're listed under **Already paid**. For each one, Bow:

- **Links** it to a transaction already in your budget, like one your bank sent, so it isn't added twice.
- **Adds** it, if it's recent and not in your budget yet.
- Or counts it as paid, so it adds to the plan's progress without changing your budget.

Check the envelope and account, then save. Bow schedules every payment still to come, and the schedule stops after the last one.

## Split a bill into payments

Paying off a bill over time, like a hospital bill? You don't need Apple Intelligence for this.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new expense">
    Tap **+**, choose **Expense**, then tap **Split Into Payments…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the payments">
    Enter the **Total Due**. Under **Split By**, choose **Payment Amount** and enter **Each Payment**, or **Number of Payments** and enter **Payments**. Choose how often it repeats and the **First Payment** date. The last payment can be smaller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule them">
    Tap **Split**. Check the envelope and account, then tap the button that reads like **Schedule 8 Payments · $380.81**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## End a schedule

Any scheduled transaction can stop on its own. When you add or edit one, turn on **Recurring**, then under **Repeats** set **Ends**:

| Ends | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Never** | It repeats until you stop it. |
| **After** | It stops after the number of **Payments** you choose. |
| **On Date** | It stops on the **End Date** you choose. |

With **After** or **On Date**, use **Already Paid** for payments you made before the schedule starts, so its progress counts the whole plan or loan.

Once a plan's last payment is entered, it moves to the **Finished** section of Scheduled Transactions. See [Scheduled transactions](/scheduled-transactions).
