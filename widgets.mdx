Bow has three widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. They only show your budget: nothing you do in a widget changes it.

| Widget | What it shows | Tap it to open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ready to Assign** | What's left to assign this month, and how many envelopes are overspent. | Budget |
| **Envelope** | What's available in the envelope you choose, and how much of its target is funded. | That envelope |
| **Scheduled Transactions** | Your next scheduled transactions for the coming weeks, soonest first. | Scheduled Transactions |

Ready to Assign and Envelope come in the small Home Screen size and the Lock Screen sizes. Scheduled Transactions comes in small and medium, and on the Lock Screen.

To choose which envelope a widget shows, touch and hold it and tap **Edit Widget**. Until you do, it shows the first envelope on Budget.

## How current they are

Widgets update when Bow runs, in front or in the background, like after a bank sync or an iCloud change. There's no Bow server to update them, so if you haven't opened Bow for a while, a widget shows when it was last updated, like **Updated Oct 28**.

The demo never appears in widgets. Before you set up your budget, or while [two budgets in iCloud](/icloud-and-backups) wait for you to keep one, they say **Open Bow to set up your budget.**

## Privacy

- With **Hide Amounts** on, widgets show no amounts at all, only **Amounts hidden**.
- With [Face ID Lock](/app-lock) on, widgets show no amounts unless you turn on **Show Amounts in Widgets** in **Settings › Privacy**.

Envelope names, payees, dates and the number of overspent envelopes still show.
