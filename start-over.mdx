---
title: "Delete your budget and start over"
description: "Erase everything and return to the welcome screen."
icon: "trash"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings › Manage Data">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete Budget and Start Over…">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a backup if you might want it back">
    Tap **Share Backup First** and save the backup. Otherwise, tap **Delete Budget…**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete Everywhere to confirm">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Every account, envelope, transaction, schedule and receipt photo is deleted, on this iPhone and on your other devices that sync with iCloud. Bank sync is disconnected and the app lock is turned off. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

Your subscription isn't affected, and settings like Appearance and Notifications stay as they are. Bow then starts fresh, as if newly installed.
