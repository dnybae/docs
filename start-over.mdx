---
title: "Delete your budget and start over"
description: "Delete your Bow budget on all your devices and start fresh, saving a backup first if you might want it back. Your subscription isn't affected."
icon: "trash"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings › Manage Data">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete Budget and Start Over…">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a backup if you might want it back">
    Tap **Share Backup First** and save the backup. Bow then asks you to confirm. Otherwise, tap **Delete Budget…**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete Everywhere to confirm">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Every account, envelope, transaction, schedule and receipt photo is deleted, on this iPhone and on your other devices that sync with iCloud, along with Recently Deleted and your custom views. Bank sync is disconnected and the app lock is turned off. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

If iCloud is off, only this iPhone's budget is deleted.

Your subscription isn't affected, and settings like Appearance and Notifications stay as they are. Bow then starts fresh, as if newly installed.
