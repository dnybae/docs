When you first open Bow, tap **Get Started**. After a few questions about you, setup has four parts: **Money**, **Envelopes**, **Targets** and **Assign**. You can go back at any step until your budget is saved, and if you leave, Bow picks up where you stopped.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Money">
    Add the accounts you spend from, with today's balances.

    - For a bank account, use your bank's **Available** balance.
    - For a credit card, use the balance including pending charges.

    Bow counts these, so don't log them again.

    If you added a card that owes money, choose how to handle that debt: pay it in full, a set amount each month, by a date, or decide later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Envelopes">
    Tap what you spend on and save for. Add your own to any group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Targets">
    Say what each essential needs this month. This is optional: tap **Skip for Now** to assign money yourself. See [Use targets](/targets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign">
    Bow funds your essentials first, in order of importance, from Ready to Assign. If there's nothing to fund, you choose how much goes where.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your budget is saved at the Assign step. To open it, start the free trial, subscribe or buy Lifetime. **Restore Purchases** and **Redeem Code** are there too. Then choose whether to turn on reminders.

After that you can add accounts, envelopes and targets anytime.

## Try it first

Tap **Try a Demo** on the welcome screen to explore a sample budget. Nothing you do there touches your own budget. When you're ready, tap **Start My Own Budget** at the top of Budget, or in Settings if you dismissed that banner.

## Coming from YNAB

Tap **Coming from YNAB?** on the welcome screen to bring your budget over instead. See [Move from YNAB](/move-from-ynab).
