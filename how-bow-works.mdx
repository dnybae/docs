Bow is an envelope budget. You decide what your money is for before you spend it, and Bow keeps track of what's left for each purpose. It's the cash-in-envelopes method, without the cash.

## The three parts

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Accounts" icon="building-columns">
    Hold your real money: checking, savings, cash and credit cards. Their balances should match your bank.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Envelopes" icon="envelope">
    What your money is for, like Rent, Groceries or Vacation. Each has an **Available** amount: what's left to spend.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ready to Assign" icon="inbox">
    Money in your accounts that isn't in an envelope yet. While there's money to assign, it shows in the bar above the tabs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Money comes in">
    Your paycheck lands in Ready to Assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You assign it">
    Move it into envelopes until Ready to Assign reaches zero. **Fund All Needs** fills every envelope's target in one tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You spend from envelopes">
    Log each purchase with the **+** button. It comes out of the envelope you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You adjust">
    If an envelope runs out, it turns red. Move money in from another one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Money you don't spend stays in its envelope and carries over to next month.
