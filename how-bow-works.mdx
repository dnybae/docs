Bow is an envelope budget. You decide what your money is for before you spend it, and Bow keeps track of what's left for each purpose. It's the cash-in-envelopes method, without the cash.

## The three parts

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Accounts" icon="building-columns">
    Hold your real money: checking, savings, cash and credit cards. Their balances should match your bank.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Envelopes" icon="envelope">
    What your money is for, like Rent, Groceries or Vacation. Each has an **Available** amount: what's left to spend.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ready to Assign" icon="inbox">
    Money in your accounts that isn't in an envelope yet. While there's money to assign, it shows in the bar above the tabs. If you've assigned more than you have, the bar turns red and reads **Over budget**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Money comes in">
    Your paycheck lands in Ready to Assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You assign it">
    Tap the Ready to Assign bar and move money into envelopes until it reaches zero. When there's enough for everything, **Fund All Needs** fills every target, covers overspending and sets aside card payments in one tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You spend from envelopes">
    Log each purchase with the **+** button. It comes out of the envelope you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You adjust">
    If an envelope goes below zero, it turns red, or orange when the overspending was on a credit card. Move money in from another one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Money you don't spend stays in its envelope and carries over to next month.

## Keep Budget tidy

- **Hide an envelope** you don't use right now: open it, tap **More** and choose **Hide Envelope**. To hide a whole group, touch and hold its name on Budget and choose **Hide Group**. Hidden envelopes wait in **Hidden Envelopes** at the bottom of Budget, where you can bring them back. An overspent envelope can't be hidden.
- **Views** filter Budget, like Overspent, Underfunded or Money Available. You can make your own.

## See the bigger picture

On Accounts, tap **Net Worth** to open **Insights**: your net worth over time, and your spending and income by envelope or by group.
