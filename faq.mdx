<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is envelope budgeting?">
    You split the money you have into envelopes, like rent, groceries and savings, before you spend it. A purchase comes out of its envelope. If an envelope has money, you can spend it. If it's empty, you move money from another one first. See [How Bow works](/how-bow-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to connect my bank?">
    No. Plenty of people enter transactions themselves, with Siri, or by scanning receipts with Apple Intelligence. You can also import a CSV, OFX or QFX file from your bank's website with [Import Bank File](/import-bank-file), in any country. If your App Store country is the US or Canada, you can connect your bank through SimpleFIN Bridge, which has its own signup and a small yearly fee. See [Connect your bank](/connect-your-bank).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my free trial ends?">
    If you keep your subscription, you're charged the amount Bow showed when you started. Bow reminds you 3 days before the first charge. If you cancel, your budget becomes read-only. It's never locked: you can still view, search, export and back it up. Bank sync and reminders pause, and restoring a backup needs a plan. See [Plans and read-only](/subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There's no Bow account and no Bow server holding your budget. Bow also keeps a backup of each of the last 14 days on your iPhone. See [iCloud sync and backups](/icloud-and-backups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Apple Intelligence?">
    No. Budgeting works the same without it. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, Bow can also fill in transactions from receipts, photos, your voice or a description, read shared receipts, bank notifications and payment plans, clean up payee names, and search your spending in your own words. See [Apple Intelligence](/apple-intelligence).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch from YNAB?">
    Yes. Choose **Coming from YNAB?** when you first open Bow and import your YNAB export. Already set up? **Settings › Import from YNAB** brings over your groups and envelopes. See [Move from YNAB](/move-from-ynab).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an iPad or Mac version?">
    Not yet. Bow is made for iPhone, including [iPhone Duo](/iphone-duo), and your budget syncs between your iPhones through iCloud.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
