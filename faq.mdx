<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is envelope budgeting?">
    You split the money you have into envelopes, like rent, groceries and savings, before you spend it. A purchase comes out of its envelope. If an envelope has money, you can spend it. If it's empty, you move money from another one first. See [How Bow works](/how-bow-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to connect my bank?">
    No. Plenty of people enter transactions themselves, with Siri, or by scanning receipts. If you want them to come in automatically, connect your bank through SimpleFIN Bridge, which has its own signup and a small yearly fee. See [Connect your bank](/connect-your-bank).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my free trial ends?">
    If you keep your subscription, you're charged the amount Bow showed when you started. If you cancel, your budget becomes read-only. It's never locked: you can still view, search, export and back it up. See [Plans and read-only](/subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There's no Bow account and no Bow server holding your budget. See [iCloud sync and backups](/icloud-and-backups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Apple Intelligence?">
    No. Budgeting works the same without it. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, Bow can also fill in transactions from receipts, photos, your voice or a description, and search your spending in your own words. See [Apple Intelligence](/apple-intelligence).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch from YNAB?">
    Yes. Choose **Coming from YNAB?** when you first open Bow and import your YNAB export. See [Move from YNAB](/move-from-ynab).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an iPad or Mac version?">
    Not yet. Bow is made for iPhone, including [iPhone Duo](/iphone-duo), and your budget syncs between your iPhones through iCloud.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
