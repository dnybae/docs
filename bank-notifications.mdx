title: "Log purchases from bank notifications"
description: "Send purchase, deposit and refund alerts from your bank or card app to Bow with a Shortcuts automation, then review them in Shared to Bow."

---

When your bank or card app sends a notification about a purchase, deposit or refund, a Shortcuts automation can send it to Bow in the background. It waits in Spending under **Shared to Bow** until you review it. With Apple Intelligence, Bow fills in the details for you; without it, you fill them in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings › Bank Notifications">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Automation to Shortcuts">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your bank apps">
    In Shortcuts, tap **App** in the automation and pick each bank or card app you use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bow picks the account from the notification: the card's last four digits, or the account it names. When you choose a different account for those digits, Bow remembers it on this iPhone. If the notification doesn't say which account, or the digits don't match any account, Account is left blank for you to choose.

The payee is the store, not your bank or card. The date is the day the notification arrived, unless it gives one.

A notification that you paid one of your cards, like “You paid CAPITAL ONE $30.36 from your Checking account”, opens as a payment to that card from the account it names, with no envelope. If more than one of your cards could be the one, choose the card yourself.

If a notification matches something already in Spending, like a transaction you entered, one from your bank or a scheduled transaction, Bow links them so it isn't added twice. It shows under **Already in your budget** for 3 days, with **Not a Match** if Bow got it wrong.

Security codes, sign-in alerts, statements, offers and declined purchases are skipped. Items you don't review within 90 days are removed.

<Tip>
  To be notified when one is waiting, turn on **New in Shared to Bow** in **Settings › Notifications**.
</Tip>
