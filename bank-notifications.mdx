---
title: "Bank notifications"
description: "Let a Shortcuts automation send purchase alerts to Bow."
icon: "bell"
---

When your bank or card app sends a notification about a purchase, deposit or refund, a Shortcuts automation can send it to Bow in the background. It waits in Spending under **Shared to Bow** until you review it. With Apple Intelligence, Bow fills in the details for you; without it, you fill them in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings › Bank Notifications">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Automation to Shortcuts">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your bank apps">
    In Shortcuts, tap **App** in the automation and pick each bank or card app you use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Security codes, sign-in alerts, statements, offers and declined purchases are skipped. Items you don't review within 90 days are removed.

<Tip>
  To be notified when one is waiting, turn on **New in Shared to Bow** in **Settings › Notifications**.
</Tip>
