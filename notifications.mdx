Bow's notifications come from your iPhone, not a server. Turn each type on in **Settings › Notifications**. All but **Bank Sync Stopped** start off, and Bow only asks for permission when you turn one on.

| Type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled Transactions** | A reminder before each upcoming scheduled expense: on the day, or 1 day, 3 days or 1 week before. Starts at 1 day before, at 9:00 AM. Dates you've skipped or already entered are left out. Reminders don't confirm whether one has been paid. |
| **Daily Check-In** | Every day at the time you choose, starting at 8:00 PM. Tells you how many bank transactions are waiting for review, or reminds you to log the day's spending. |
| **New in Shared to Bow** | Right away when a bank notification lands in Shared to Bow. See [Bank notifications](/bank-notifications). |
| **Weekly Check-In** | On the day and time you choose. Starts on Sunday at 6:00 PM. |
| **Bank Sync Stopped** | On from the start. If your bank hasn't synced for 3 days, the iPhone connected to your bank lets you know, even when Bow isn't open. It's delivered once notifications are allowed for Bow. |

During setup, the **Stay on track** step offers the daily and weekly check-ins. They're only turned on if you tap **Turn On Reminders**.

While your budget is [read-only](/subscriptions) or you're in the demo, Bow sends no budget notifications.

## Trial reminder

If you're on a free trial that's set to renew, Bow reminds you at 10:00 AM, 3 days before the first charge, with the plan, date and price. It only does this if notifications are already allowed.

## Privacy

Payees and amounts are left out of notifications unless you turn on **Show Financial Details** under Privacy. It applies to scheduled transaction reminders and New in Shared to Bow, which may appear on your Lock Screen. With **Hide Amounts** on, notifications show dots instead of amounts.
