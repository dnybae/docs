Bow works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. Say any of these phrases, or find Bow's actions in Shortcuts.

<Tip>
  Siri and Shortcuts still work while Bow is [locked with Face ID](/app-lock).
</Tip>

## Log spending

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| “Log an expense in Bow” | Siri saves it right away, with Undo. |
| “Add to Bow” | Describe it in your own words, like “Paycheck from Acme 2,500”. Bow fills it in and records it. Needs Apple Intelligence. |
| “Add a subscription to Bow” | Describe a bill, like “Netflix 15.49 a month on the 3rd”. Bow fills it in and schedules it. Needs Apple Intelligence. |

## Check your budget

| Say | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| “How much is left in Groceries in Bow” | What's available in an envelope. |
| “What's ready to assign in Bow” | Money not in an envelope yet. |
| “How much did I spend on Dining in Bow” | Spending for an envelope or a period. |
| “What bills are coming up in Bow” | Your upcoming scheduled bills. |
| “What's my Checking balance in Bow” | An account's balance. |
| “Open Groceries in Bow” | Opens that envelope in Bow. |

## Ask Bow

Say “Ask Bow” and ask a question about your budget in your own words, like “Am I overspent anywhere?”. Ask Bow needs Apple Intelligence turned on in Bow's settings.

<Note>
  Siri's answers say amounts out loud, even with **Hide Amounts** turned on.
</Note>
