Bow works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. Say any of these phrases, or find Bow's actions in Shortcuts.

<Tip>
  While Bow is [locked with Face ID](/app-lock), Siri still logs spending but doesn't read your budget. Questions and Ask Bow answer that Face ID Lock is on, and Spotlight and Shortcuts don't list your transactions.
</Tip>

## Log spending

| Say | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| “Log an expense in Bow” | Siri asks for each detail and saves it right away. The card that follows has **Undo** and **Change Envelope**. |
| “Add to Bow” | Describe it in your own words, like “Paycheck from Acme 2,500”. It waits in Spending under Shared to Bow, filled in, for you to review and save. |
| “Add a subscription to Bow” | Describe a subscription, like “Netflix 15.49 a month on the 3rd”. It waits in Spending for you to review and schedule. |

With Apple Intelligence, Bow fills in what you said. Without it, the item still waits in Spending and you fill in the details yourself.

## Check your budget

| Say | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| “How much is left in Groceries in Bow” | What's available in an envelope. |
| “What's ready to assign in Bow” | Money not in an envelope yet. |
| “How much did I spend on Dining in Bow” | Spending for an envelope or a period. |
| “What’s coming up in Bow” | Your upcoming scheduled transactions. |
| “What's my Checking balance in Bow” | An account's balance. |
| “Open Groceries in Bow” | Opens that envelope in Bow. |

## Ask Bow

Say “Ask Bow” and ask a question about your budget in your own words, like “Am I overspent anywhere?”. Ask Bow needs Apple Intelligence turned on in Bow's settings.

## More in Shortcuts

The Shortcuts app also has **Open Account**, **Open Transaction**, **Check Overspending** and **Send to Bow for Review**, which [bank notifications](/bank-notifications) use.

<Note>
  Siri's answers say amounts out loud, even with **Hide Amounts** turned on. Transaction lists in Shortcuts and Spotlight show $••• instead.
</Note>
