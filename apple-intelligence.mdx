<Info>
  You don't need Apple Intelligence to use Bow. Budgeting works the same without it, and iPhones without it don't show these buttons.
</Info>

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, Bow can fill in transactions for you and search your spending in your own words. Apple Intelligence does the reading on your iPhone. Bow uses only Apple's own frameworks, with no third-party models or servers.

## Fill In with Bow

When you add an expense or inflow, tap **✨** next to **Add**, then choose how to fill it in:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Scan a receipt" icon="receipt">
    Point your camera at a paper receipt. The receipt is kept as the transaction's photo.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose a photo" icon="image">
    Pick a receipt or screenshot from Photos.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speak" icon="microphone">
    Say what you bought, out loud.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Describe" icon="keyboard">
    Type it in your own words, like “Chipotle, 14.25, yesterday”.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Bow fills in the transaction sheet and you check everything before it's added. It never saves on its own.

## Shared to Bow

Share receipt photos, screenshots and PDFs to Bow from any app and choose **Add to Bow**. They wait at the top of Spending under **Shared to Bow** until you review them. With Apple Intelligence, Bow fills in the details; without it, you fill them in.

You can also send purchase alerts from your bank's app, and Bow reads [payment plan](/payment-plans) screenshots. See [Bank notifications](/bank-notifications).

## Ask about your spending

Say “Ask Bow” to Siri, then ask in your own words, like “How much did I spend on takeout last month?” or “Am I overspent anywhere?”. See [Siri and Shortcuts](/siri-and-shortcuts).

In Spending, type something like “pet care last month” and tap **Find**. Bow finds related purchases, even without a matching envelope or note.

The numbers in every answer come from Bow's own calculations, never from the model.

## Clean up payee names

In **Settings › Manage Payees**, tap **Clean Up Payee Names** to turn bank text like “SQ *BLUE BTL 0423” into Blue Bottle Coffee, merging payees that are the same place. The names your bank uses are kept, so bank sync still recognizes them.

## Turn it on or off

**Settings › Apple Intelligence** has a switch for all of these, and lists what each one does.
