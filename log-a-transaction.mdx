<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap + at the bottom right">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the amount">
    Digits fill in from the right, so `1250` is 12.50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Expense, Inflow, Transfer or Payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the payee">
    For an inflow, choose the source. Bow fills in the account you used with them last time and the envelope you usually choose for them. One different choice doesn't change that envelope; two of your last three do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and add">
    Check the envelope, account and date, then tap **Add** at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you pick a future date, the button says **Schedule**, and Bow adds it as a scheduled transaction.

If an expense will leave its envelope overspent, Bow tells you before you add it. You can still add it and [cover the envelope](/overspending) afterward.

## Types

| Type | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Expense** | Money you spend, taken from an envelope. |
| **Inflow** | Money coming in. Most goes to Ready to Assign; a refund can go back to an envelope. |
| **Transfer** | Money between two of your accounts. Transfers between on-budget accounts don't count as spending. |
| **Payment** | Paying a credit card from another account. See [Credit cards](/credit-cards). |

## More ways to add

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Split between envelopes" icon="table-columns">
    In the envelope list, tap **+** and choose **Split Between Envelopes**. Useful for a store run that's part groceries and part household.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Keep a receipt photo" icon="camera">
    Tap **Add Photo** to keep a receipt with the transaction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Make it recurring" icon="repeat">
    Turn on **Recurring** to make it a scheduled transaction, like a bill or paycheck. It can repeat forever, or end after a number of payments or on a date. See [End a schedule](/payment-plans#end-a-schedule).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add a payment plan" icon="calendar-check">
    Share a Klarna, Affirm or Afterpay screenshot and Bow schedules each payment. See [Payment plans](/payment-plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fill In with Bow" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, scan a receipt, choose a photo, speak or describe the purchase, and Bow fills in the sheet for you to check. See [Apple Intelligence](/apple-intelligence).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Share from another app" icon="share-from-square">
    Share a receipt photo, screenshot or PDF from another app and choose **Add to Bow**. It waits at the top of Spending under **Shared to Bow**, and Bow keeps the receipt with the transaction you add.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask Siri" icon="microphone">
    Say “Log an expense in Bow”. Siri saves it right away, with Undo. See [Siri and Shortcuts](/siri-and-shortcuts).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
