Bow keeps a way back for the changes that matter most: moving money, editing a transaction and deleting something.

## Undo money moves

Tap **⋯** on Budget and choose **Undo** or **Redo**. Each names the step it will take back, like an assignment or a move. They cover money moves made since you opened Bow: assigning, moving, covering overspending and Budget's actions, newest first.

## Recent Moves

**Recent Moves** lists every assignment and move across your budget, newest first, grouped by day. Open it from Budget's **⋯** menu or **Settings › Budget**. Use **Show** at the top to see all of them, only moves, or only assignments. To take one back, swipe left on it and tap **Undo**.

## Undo an edit

After you save a change to a transaction or a scheduled transaction, or approve a bank transaction, the message at the bottom offers **Undo** for a few seconds. If the same records changed since, on this iPhone or another device, Undo changes nothing and tells you.

## Recently Deleted

What you delete stays in **Settings › Recently Deleted** for 30 days: transactions, scheduled transactions, envelopes, groups, accounts, payees and views. After that, it's deleted for good.

- To bring one back, tap it and choose **Restore**, or swipe right on it. It comes back on all your devices. If it needs something you deleted after it, like the account a transaction was in, Bow says so and they come back together.
- To delete one for good now, swipe left on it and tap **Delete Now**. **Delete All** empties the list.

Restoring changes your budget, so it needs a plan. While your budget is [read-only](/subscriptions), you can still delete items for good.

<Note>
  [Delete Budget and Start Over](/start-over) empties Recently Deleted too.
</Note>
